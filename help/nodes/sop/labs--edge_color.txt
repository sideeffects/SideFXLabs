= Labs Edge Color =

""" Color sharp edges on geometry """

This node highlights sharp changes in geometry, with control over contrast and blur. It also lets you identify convex vs concave edges, and treat them separately. 

NOTE:
    Replaced by Labs Measure Curvature SOP 3.2. To be removed in Houdini 24.0.

@parameters

    Mask Color:
	    #id: color
        Color to use for edges.

    Blend Mode:
        #id: blend_mode
        Method to blend the colors.

    == Convex  ==

    Intensity:
        #id: concave_range_scale
        Intensity to mask convex edges.

    Contrast:
        #id: concave_contrast
        How much contrast to add, making edge masking appear thinner or thicker.

    Blur Amount:
        #id: concave_blur_ammount
        Amount to blur and soften the result.

    == Concave ==
    
    Intensity:
        #id: convex_range_scale
        Intensity to mask concave edges.

    Contrast:
        #id: convex_contrast
        How much contrast to add, making edge masking appear thinner or thicker.

    Blur Amount:
        #id: convex_blur_ammount
        Amount to blur and soften the result.
